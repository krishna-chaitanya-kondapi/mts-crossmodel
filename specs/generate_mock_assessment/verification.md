# Verification and historical regression cases

## Verification gates

| Gate | Evidence |
|---|---|
| G1 human pre-run | Approved resolved scope, blueprint, source and presentation policy, preflight and estimated runtime |
| Q1 content | Every item independently solved or reviewed, key/rubric comparison, counts, standards, source fidelity, question validity |
| Q2 structure | Manifest/PDF/key parity, numbering, page count and size, references, PDF integrity |
| Q3 visual | Every page inspected, diagrams and all labels intact, minimum scale/clearance, no clipping or overlap, readability |
| G2 human release | Exact candidate artifacts, evidence, explicit exceptions and approval; verify approved identity on publication |

Separate generation and review. Record PASS, FAIL, NOT_VERIFIED or not applicable with evidence and method. AI judgment is not deterministic proof. Repair affected items/layout, repeat relevant checks and regressions, escalate persistent failures. Recompute every Math answer after edits. ELA line references must match actual presented passage; correct inaccurate source references with traceability. Verify defensible best answer and textual support; document source discrepancies. Missing figures, ambiguous options, unsupported score extrapolation, unreadable compression and unauthorized release are blocking concerns. Do not bypass verification for official sources.
