# Approved Definition of Done

Initial milestone: reproduce historical source-based Math and ELA benchmarks with educational, functional and presentation equivalence. Approved November 18, 2026. Autonomous implementation, testing and repair within scope. Independent product architecture is mandatory: do not modify or depend on weekly generation, curriculum allocation, weekly prompts or weekly presentation configuration. Reuse only product-neutral primitives. No production publication without human approval.

Registry: 78 criteria, 76 mandatory. V02.02 and V02.03 are deferred broad product features; selected benchmark purposes and lengths remain required. No mandatory waivers or exclusions in this initial milestone without an explicit approved registry revision. A passing machine gate means ready for human acceptance, not final acceptance.

Continue repair and verification while actionable work and execution capacity remain. Persist evidence, defects and checkpoints; stop NOT_DONE at resource limits or genuine blockers. Do not claim reproduction by copying a golden PDF. Verify every question independently and inspect every page. Evidence must bind exact revision and actual files; hashes establish identity, not educational correctness.

Deliver executable workflow, golden RunSpecs, regenerated worksheets/keys, tests, traceability, reports in Markdown/JSON and logs. See [registry](acceptance-criteria.json), [checkpoint](implementation-checkpoint.json), [inventory](golden-reference-inventory.md).
