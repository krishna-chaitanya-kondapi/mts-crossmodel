# Mock assessment layout issues and resolution plan

**Status** Open · Phase 3 layout review  
**Workflow branch** `mock-assessment-verification-progress`  
**Scope** 2022 New York Math and ELA student booklets, Grades 4–6

## Math · Large gaps around wide items

**Evidence.** Grade 4 and Grade 6 page 1 leave avoidable space inside the two-column body. In Grade 6, the short Q1 sits opposite the taller Q2, then Q3's full-width prism net leaves the facing area empty. The 2024 samples show how diagrams and other questions can share a page without these interruptions. Both the current and comparison Grade 4 and Grade 6 files have four pages. Since their selections differ, judge page savings by comparing the current and revised 2022 files.

**Current run.** Start with both reported first pages, then audit every Math page. Try fitting each figure and its question into one column while preserving the complete source figure, labels and readability. Rebalance the columns using actual block heights, then check if later complete questions can move forward in reading order. Keep working room, session boundaries and the four-page maximum. The current baseline is 4, 3 and 4 pages for Grades 4, 5 and 6.

**Future runs.** Add a Phase 3 check for interior and trailing blank regions in each column. Flag a gap if a complete following item can fit in order at the approved size. Test a short item beside a taller item followed by a diagram, using the reference PDFs for visual comparison. Record each retained full-width item and why it needs the space.

## ELA · Unused bottom space before page breaks

**Evidence.** The current Grade 4, 5 and 6 booklets run 7, 5 and 6 pages. Examples to audit include Grade 4 pages 1, 3 and 5, Grade 5 pages 2–3, and Grade 6 pages 2–4. Earlier verification checked that columns were populated, but did not test if the next complete block could fit.

**Current run.** Flow the next complete question or passage block into available space when the transition remains clear. Continue across pages as needed. Keep headings with opening text, keep every multiple-choice item with all its choices, preserve the table, diagram and poem line markers, and keep credit-appropriate writing space. Record before-and-after page counts and the reason for remaining large gaps.

**Future runs.** Use roughly 30% unused body area as a review trigger. Test whether following content can move back at the approved typography and spacing. Record deliberate response space, a justified new-passage break or the final page when applicable. Add regression cases for questions following a short passage, figure placement, poetry line references and full-width responses.

## Next review handoff

Return revised PDFs and a page-by-page before/after report. Show the items that moved, any remaining exceptions, page counts by grade and subject, and renewed checks for source fidelity, key alignment, two columns, clipping and readability. Keep Phase 4 release pending human approval.
